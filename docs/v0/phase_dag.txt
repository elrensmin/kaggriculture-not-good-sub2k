  opening cash committed
     -> animals on board                 the same bank buys the opening herd -- you cannot fund both fully
     -> MELON tiles                      the d0-d2 MELON block is bought with opening cash
     -> quadrants unlocked               the same opening bank buys land; the #1 spends to $6 and takes NE on d6
  quadrants unlocked
     -> owned tiles                      a quadrant is 25 tiles
     -> owned tiles                      NE at d6 is worth 25 tiles for the whole strawberry ramp
     -> quadrants unlocked               land is bought in order, one quadrant at a time
  owned tiles
     -> animals on board                 ANIMAL TILE = CROP TILE at 25 tiles: measured, a 6-tile holdback + a 6-animal herd cost $15,020 vs 25-tile crops (margin, matched pairs, p=0.0001)
     -> empty owned tiles                more ground with the same crew leaves tiles bare
     -> hands                            hands are hired to ground: min(schedule, owned//TILES_PER_HAND)
     -> STRAWBERRY tiles                 the ramp can only claim tiles we own
  animals on board
     -> animals on board                 phase 1's herd is the floor for phase 2's herd
     -> CARE ops                         care is free and daily, and it banks the bonus
     -> FEED ops                         one wheat per animal per day
     -> idle share %                     feed/care/collect are DAILY work; a crop-only farm has nothing to do on the odd days
     -> animal structures                housing is built only for animals already owned
  MELON tiles
     -> WATER ops                        melon's water window is ages 6-12, so it drives early demand
  STRAWBERRY tiles
     -> WATER ops                        ongoing crops need watering to keep producing
  PLANT ops
     -> idle share %                     planting is the only other filling work
     -> WATER ops                        a newly planted tile must be watered the same day or it weeds that night
  idle share %
     -> empty owned tiles                idle crew + bare tiles = the ramp is throttling planting
  owned tiles
     -> animals on board                 the herd is tile-blocked: an animal tile is a crop tile
     -> hands                            hands are capped by ground
     -> STRAWBERRY tiles                 the strawberry ramp peaks at 30 tiles
     -> TOMATO tiles                     the third rotation also needs room
     -> WHEAT tiles                      tiles are the ceiling on every crop ramp (C4)
  hands
     -> idle share %                     a crew bigger than the work idles
     -> WATER ops                        the crew is the watering capacity
  animals on board
     -> animals at the bell              the herd at the bell is the herd we fed to the end
     -> COLLECT_FERTILIZER ops           one fertilizer per animal per day
     -> FEED ops                         one per animal per day
     -> FERTILIZE ops                    fertilizer supply gates FERTILIZE
     -> idle share %                     daily feed/care/collect work
     -> shed peak                        milk/wool/egg/fertilizer also land in the shed (C1)
     -> animal structures                housing follows animals
  WHEAT tiles
     -> FEED ops                         home-grown wheat is the cheap feed (C2)
     -> WATER ops                        wheat's window is ages 2-4
  STRAWBERRY tiles
     -> WATER ops                        watering demand scales with standing crops
  FERTILIZE ops
     -> FERTILIZE ops                    the fertilizer chain carries over
     -> HARVEST ops                      fertilizer doubles one-shot yield and ongoing fruit
     -> idle share %                     the fertilize/pickup chain is more daily work
  COLLECT_FERTILIZER ops
     -> FERTILIZE ops                    the pickup -> fertilize chain
  WATER ops
     -> plants died                      an unwatered plant weeds after two nights (C5)
     -> WATER ops                        the same watering demand carries into the last days
     -> weeds                            watering is what keeps a tile from becoming a weed
  WATER ops per planted tile
     -> plants died                      coverage, not raw watering, is what keeps plants alive
  plants died
     -> HARVEST ops                      a dead plant is a harvest that never happens
     -> weeds                            a dead plant IS a weed
  HARVEST ops
     -> HARVEST ops                      standing crops at d18 are what d18-29 can harvest
     -> shed peak                        harvests land in the shed
  weeds
     -> HARVEST ops                      weed tiles have to be dug before they can be replanted
     -> weeds                            weeds only accumulate
  shed peak
     -> idle share %                     a full shed turns harvest into DROP/PICKUP walking
  planted tiles
     -> WATER ops                        every planted tile is watering demand
  animals at the bell
     -> idle share %                     the herd is the daily work in the endgame too
  weeds
     -> HARVEST ops                      a weeded tile cannot be harvested
  WATER ops
     -> weeds                            retiring a tile means it weeds -- deliberately (rule #4)
  HARVEST ops
     -> HARVEST per planted tile         throughput against what was standing
  shed at the bell
     -> idle share %                     carrying to a full shed costs turns
  HARVEST per planted tile
     -> shed at the bell                 harvests that cannot be sold accumulate in the shed
  shops unlocked
     -> YARN_STORE unlocked              YARN_STORE is one of the shops in the draw
  YARN_STORE unlocked
     -> animals on board                 MEASURED, LAGGED: at the season end YARN worlds hold SHEEP 7.5 / GOOSE 2 while no-YARN worlds hold SHEEP 0 / GOOSE 6 -- but his d5 mix is invariant, so this edge acts from d6 on, never in the opening
     -> STRAWBERRY tiles                 a shop also changes which crop pays
