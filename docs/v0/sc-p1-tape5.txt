    owner: src/herd_plan.py::market_intents
    explains 4 other deficient metric(s): CARE ops, empty owned tiles, FEED ops, idle share %
    downstream mechanisms:

      -> FEED ops: one wheat per animal per day

      -> animal structures: housing is built only for animals already owned

    downstream mechanisms:
      -> idle share %: planting is the only other filling work
      -> WATER ops: a newly planted tile must be watered the same day or it weeds that night
